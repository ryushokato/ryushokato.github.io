About

I am a graduate student at the Graduate School of Energy Science, Kyoto University.

My research focuses on energy security in the transition toward hydrogen-based energy systems, particularly the impacts of hydrogen deployment on energy import structures, supply diversification, and energy-system resilience.

Research

Hydrogen Energy Security

The transition toward hydrogen energy may substantially change existing fossil-fuel demand and international energy trade structures. My research investigates how hydrogen deployment affects Japan's energy import structure and energy security.

Current research interests include:

  • Hydrogen energy security
  • Energy import diversification
  • Energy supply resilience
  • Energy policy
  • Energy-system analysis
